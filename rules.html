DIRECTIVE PROTOCOL

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Adherence to the following directives is mandatory for all competitors participating in CYBERTRIX CTF 2026.

01. STANDARD FLAG SYNTAX

Unless explicitly instructed otherwise inside a challenge briefing, all captured flags adhere to the standard CYBERTRIX format:

CYBERTRIX{s0m3_s3cr3t_fl4g_h3r3}

Flags are strictly case-sensitive. Ensure no leading or trailing whitespace is accidentally submitted.

02. SCORING & TIE-BREAKER LOGIC

  • Points are awarded instantaneously upon first successful flag submission.
  • Duplicate submissions for the same challenge will not award additional points.
  • In the event of a points tie, ranking priority is determined by:
    1. Highest number of distinct solved challenges.
    2. Earliest timestamp of the last accepted submission.

03. PROHIBITED ACTIONS (ZERO TOLERANCE)

  • Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks against the CYBERTRIX platform infrastructure or fellow competitors are strictly forbidden.
  • Brute forcing / automated directory fuzzing of the main platform web interface or submission endpoints is prohibited and will trigger automatic rate-limiting bans.
  • Flag sharing or collusion between competing individuals or squads is grounds for immediate disqualification.
  • Attacking any target, IP, or network outside of designated sandbox challenge environments is strictly illegal.

04. ASSISTANCE & ADJUDICATION

If you encounter a potential bug or infrastructure issue with a challenge, notify the challenge organizers immediately. The decisions of the CYBERTRIX Admin Panel are final.
